Important identity and coverage decisions

  • The mixed violin-concerto recording at 3548–3554 contains Saint-Saëns No. 3, Op. 61, and Glazunov’s Op. 82, not two Sinding concertos (performing label).
  • 3912–3914 are Mozart’s Symphony No. 32, K. 318, used as an overture to Zaide. The Chandos track list corrects the legacy K. 344 assignment. The existing recommendation is moved to the correct Work, retaining its Performance ID.
  • Keep Schumann’s 1841 and 1851 Fourth Symphony versions distinct within one Work Group. Keep suites distinct from complete ballets, including Hindemith’s Nobilissima visione, Schmitt’s original 1907 La Tragédie de Salomé, Busoni’s Die Brautwahl suite, and Prokofiev’s independent suites.
  • Preserve Faure/Aubert’s Fantaisie, Lutosławski’s 1955 clarinet/chamber-orchestra second version, Richter’s Recomposed and its separate five-piece Shadows, Ullén’s four Infinite Bach recompositions, and Ravel/Hesketh’s published Fugue and Toccata.
  • Grove explicitly distinguishes Stravinsky’s 1941 Bluebird pas-de-deux orchestration from the 1921 Variation d’Aurore and Entr’acte orchestrations. Those six selected tracks become three derived Works in the Sleeping Beauty family (performing ensemble).
  • Saint-Saëns’s eight-track Mélodies persanes selection is the Palazzetto Bru Zane orchestral edition, interleaving excerpts from La Nuit persane, rather than the unqualified original six-song piano cycle (institutional record). Respighi’s Nebbie is publicly marked as the selected middle song of the original orchestral Tre liriche, P. 99a; it is not assigned to the later Di Vittorio completed edition.
  • Delius’s source names poets rather than performers. The matching 2015 Chandos reissue establishes Hickox, the Bournemouth forces, Bryn Terfel and Sally Burgess with appropriate Work coverage. Poets and composers are not imported as performers merely from an album-level artist field.
  • Parent opera/choral Works keep public excerpt descriptions for selected arias, overtures and movements. Non-contiguous movements and additional short edits remain explicit occurrence/coverage evidence rather than new compositions.
  • Two performances do not create two Works. Reconcile bilingual title variants of Roussel’s First Symphony and keep its two selections pending. The two Galanta recordings, two Naughty Limericks recordings and other competing selections likewise require a choice.
  • Track 4000 repeats the ISRC of the earlier Diepenbrock candidate at 1086. The competing selection at 1082 is still unresolved. Repeating one candidate in this window does not choose it automatically; the earlier review remains open.
