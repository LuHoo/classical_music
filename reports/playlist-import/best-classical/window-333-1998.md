## Identity and coverage corrections

- BIS-2657 distinguishes Bartók’s 1929 / 1935 violin-orchestra rhapsody revisions and Martinů’s complete 1944 H. 276 II suite from the Méditation excerpt of the 1939 H. 276 I version. The booklet track table takes precedence over its contradictory narrative numbering.
- Positions 899–901 are Fanny Hensel songs, orchestrated by Sándor Balogh, rather than Felix Mendelssohn compositions. Suk’s Fantastic Scherzo is Op. 25 and his early symphony is in E major, correcting provider errors.
- Weill’s three Berberian songs are independent Berio arrangements, supported by the Weill Foundation / Berio Centre. They are not presented as recordings of complete stage works. The string-orchestra Shaker Loops, Ravel’s orchestral pieces, Mussorgsky/Ravel Pictures, and the Waterman, Schneemann, Lascae, Seidl and Sitt arrangements retain specific Work identities.
- Bruckner Symphony No. 1 reuses the exact Poschner recording, with a corrected **1868 Linz version** assignment from Capriccio C8092. Existing Bach arrangement records now identify Stokowski, Elgar, Webern, Schoenberg and Mahler explicitly; the Ricercar title specifies the selected artistic object. Concatenated Salonen artist text was separated into actual performers. The existing Mozart Great Mass Work now explicitly identifies Beyer’s 1989 completion from BIS-2171.
- Partial selections carry public excerpts: Martinů H. 276 I; Saint-Saëns Samson Bacchanale; Bernstein Candide overture; Rameau Castor et Pollux aria; Dohnányi Tante Simona overture; Mendelssohn Symphonies 1 and 3; Chopin Piano Concerto 1; Diepenbrock The Birds overture; Rachmaninoff All-Night Vigil No. 5; Massenet Thaïs Méditation; and Tchaikovsky Symphony 3, whose third movement is absent.
- Grieg’s four Lyric Suite movements occupy positions 1531 and 1533–1535 and form one complete recommendation. Bell Ringing at 1532 is a different orchestration. Rana’s Liszt bonus appears on the same Warner release in Atmos and stereo with different format ISRCs; one piano performance is retained, without the album’s orchestra/conductor credits.
- General Works retain explicit `version_assignment: unspecified` where the recording evidence does not identify a specific revision: Walton’s Viola Concerto, Barber’s Symphony 2, Rameau’s Castor excerpt, Mendelssohn Symphonies 4/5, and relevant Tchaikovsky revised works. No completion or independently arranged work is hidden by this fallback.
- The inherited Mozart opera wind-arrangement recommendations retain their existing, explicit `wind arrangement` profiles and notes. This batch does not reclassify that older modelling convention.
